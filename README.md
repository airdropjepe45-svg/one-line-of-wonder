One line, at most 200 characters, written to make a reader feel wonder.

## The line

A crack in Botswana, 180 million years old, stirred back to life in
2017 — and the ground shook.

The fact is real and verified from the news research of 24 November
2026: Botswana's 2017 earthquake was traced to a crack 180 million
years old stirring back to life. A line in the rock, older than
flowers, older than birds — and it woke up.

## How to render

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` is a self-contained Node 22 program (stdlib only, no network).
It writes the line to `entry.txt` — one line, UTF-8, deterministic.
